# @sydneysynced Hook Style Bank

Purpose: use these as STYLE REFERENCES for DBT-Mind / DBT / BPD TikTok slideshow hooks.
Do NOT copy exact hooks. Preserve architecture and swap only the niche payload.

STYLE-PRESERVING ADAPTATION RULES
- Preserve skeletons: `how to... WITHOUT...`, `5 things...`, `5 rules...`, `HOW TO...: subtitle`, `[hook] (parenthetical)`.
- Preserve punctuation/casing when useful: all-caps emphasis like ACTUALLY, WITHOUT, INSTANTLY, MUST, UNRECOGNIZABLE.
- Preserve short parentheticals when useful: (realistically), (you’re not alone), (backed by science), (before you text back).
- Change only the subject matter into DBT/BPD/relationship-anxiety language.
- Prefer broad relationship/self-worth language first, quiet DBT underneath.
- Do not turn these into clinical hooks like “DBT skills for emotional regulation”.
